Privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

Mo2IT Technologies (“we”) is a private company. This policy explains how we handle your personal information under South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

What we collect

  • Your name, mobile number and email address.
  • Your company name, if you add it to your profile.
  • What you tell us in quote requests, booking requests and messages.
  • Sign-in details if you create a profile. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase and we cannot see them. If you sign up with Google, we receive your name and email address.
  • How many times you have signed in and when you last did.

Why we use it

To reply to you, prepare quotes, arrange bookings, deliver our services and keep your account working. We do not sell your information.

Who processes it

We use Google Firebase to store accounts, requests and messages, and GitHub Pages to host this website. These providers may process information, such as your IP address, on servers outside South Africa. If you contact us by email or WhatsApp, those providers also process your message.

Payments

This website does not take payments or collect card details.

Browser storage

If you sign in, your browser stores what is needed to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep information while we need it for your request or project, or while the law requires it. You can ask us to delete your profile and messages.

Security
